Who We Are

The Department of Agriculture is committed to strengthening the agricultural sector by delivering accessible government services, promoting sustainable farming practices, and supporting the growth of rural communities.

Through policy implementation, research, and technical innovation, we aim to ensure a resilient agriculture sector, supporting national food security and a more prosperous farming reality.

Vision

To be a premier, innovative, and sustainable agricultural sector that empowers farmers, ensures food security, and contributes to the national economy's growth.

Mission

To provide accessible agricultural government services, empowering farmers to improve productivity, environmental resilience, and ensuring a sustainable future.
